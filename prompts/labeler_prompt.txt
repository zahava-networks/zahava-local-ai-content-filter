HALACHIC STANDARDS (apply these EXACTLY — Orthodox interpretation)
================================================================================

An image is NOT ACCEPTABLE (block=true) if ANY of the following are true.
Apply these rules identically to PHOTOS, CARTOONS, ILLUSTRATIONS, ANIME, DRAWINGS.
Apply these rules identically to ADULTS and CHILDREN.

  - Sleeves end at or above the elbow → NOT ACCEPTABLE
  - Sleeves must extend PAST the elbow joint to be acceptable
  - Skirt/dress hem ends at or above the kneecap → NOT ACCEPTABLE
  - Hem must end clearly BELOW the kneecap (mid-calf or longer) to be acceptable
  - Any visible cleavage → NOT ACCEPTABLE
  - Pants, shorts, leggings, bodycon, tight jeans, fitted athletic wear on women → NOT ACCEPTABLE
    (women wear skirts/dresses; pants are not modest dress in this standard)
  - Tight or form-fitting clothing that hugs body contours → NOT ACCEPTABLE
    even if the clothing technically covers the right areas. Leggings, bodycon
    dresses, fitted athletic wear, tight jeans all qualify as "tight."
  - Swimwear, bikinis, beachwear, exposed-midriff athletic wear → NOT ACCEPTABLE
  - Any visible nudity, including partial → NOT ACCEPTABLE

MEN AND BOYS:
  - Shirtless in ANY context (sports, beach, swimming, anywhere) → NOT ACCEPTABLE
  - Any visible nudity → NOT ACCEPTABLE
  - (Men's sleeve and pants standards are less strict and not enforced by this filter)

BOTH GENDERS, ALL AGES:
  - Romantic or intimate physical contact between a man and a woman:
    kissing, hugging in a romantic way, cuddling, suggestive poses
    → NOT ACCEPTABLE
    (handshakes, standing side-by-side, casual non-romantic contact are FINE)
  - Suggestive poses regardless of clothing → NOT ACCEPTABLE
  - Any visible nudity → NOT ACCEPTABLE

ERROR PREFERENCE:
  - When in doubt, BLOCK. False positives (over-blocking) are acceptable.
    False negatives (letting non-modest content through) are NOT acceptable.

KEY DEFINITIONS — apply EXACTLY
================================================================================

sleeve_length:
  - "none"           : sleeveless / strapless / spaghetti strap / tank top
  - "short"          : sleeve ends clearly above the elbow joint (NOT acceptable for women)
  - "elbow"          : sleeve ends at or just covering the elbow joint (NOT acceptable for women — must extend past)
  - "three_quarter"  : sleeve ends clearly past the elbow, above the wrist (acceptable)
  - "long"           : sleeve reaches or passes the wrist (acceptable)
  - "not_visible"    : arms are out of frame, completely occluded, or behind the body

neckline:
  - "modest"           : neckline covers all cleavage and collarbone area appropriately
  - "cleavage_visible" : any cleavage visible (NOT acceptable)
  - "no_top"           : person has no top garment at all
  - "not_visible"      : neckline area is occluded or out of frame

lower_garment:
  - "skirt"      : skirt or dress
  - "pants"      : trousers, jeans, leggings, joggers — anything covering each leg separately (NOT acceptable for women)
  - "shorts"     : pants ending above the knee (NOT acceptable)
  - "swimwear"   : bikini, one-piece swimsuit, board shorts as swimwear (NOT acceptable)
  - "underwear"  : visible underwear/lingerie (NOT acceptable)
  - "none"       : no lower-body garment (NOT acceptable)
  - "not_visible": lower body is out of frame or fully occluded

lower_length (applies to skirts and pants):
  - "above_knee"    : hem ends above the kneecap (NOT acceptable for skirts on women)
  - "at_knee"       : hem ends at the kneecap (NOT acceptable — must be clearly below)
  - "below_knee"    : hem ends clearly below the kneecap, mid-calf or lower (acceptable)
  - "full"          : hem reaches ankle or floor (acceptable)
  - "not_visible"   : lower body occluded

fit:
  - "loose"       : clothing hangs loosely, body contours not visible
  - "fitted"      : clothing follows body shape moderately but is NOT skin-tight
  - "tight"       : clothing hugs body contours, skin-tight, bodycon, leggings, fitted
                    athletic wear — even if technically covering. THIS IS NOT ACCEPTABLE.
  - "not_visible" : body silhouette is occluded or only head/face visible

visible_nudity:
  - "none"    : no skin areas violating tzniut beyond hands/face/feet
  - "partial" : some private area visible without full exposure (e.g., visible bra,
                exposed midriff, side-boob, etc.)
  - "full"    : nude, topless, fully exposed private areas

shirtless_male: true if a male person is visibly shirtless. ANY shirtless male is NOT acceptable.

romantic_contact: true if a man and a woman are engaged in kissing, hugging in a clearly
romantic way, intimate physical contact, or any pose suggesting romantic/sexual intimacy.
Hand-holding by a couple counts as romantic_contact. Family hugs, handshakes, side-by-side
standing, professional photos do NOT count.

suggestive_pose: true if the pose is sexually suggestive regardless of clothing
(provocative posture, sultry expression with body angled, etc.). Be conservative —
flag if you would describe it as sexual or suggestive to a stranger.
